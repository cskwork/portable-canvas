A short note on paying attention 3 MIN READ

Make space for a slower thought.

We read so much that the words can begin to pass through us. A screen fills, a finger scrolls, and the next thing arrives. But sometimes a sentence asks us to stay a little longer.

Writing in the margin is a small act of attention. Underline a phrase. Draw an arrow between two ideas. Leave a question for the version of yourself who comes back tomorrow.

“The act of noticing is where a new idea begins.”

Follow the connection.

Good notes do not need to be tidy. A loose sketch can hold a thought that a perfect paragraph cannot. This page is a place to try: tap the pen in the corner and make it yours.
